Whisper is a speech recognition model developed by OpenAI. It can transcribe audio into text and supports multiple languages.

WavLM is a speech representation model. It can learn useful audio features for tasks such as speaker recognition and speech classification.

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It retrieves relevant information from external documents before an LLM generates an answer.

Vector databases store embeddings. Embeddings are numerical representations of meaning that allow semantically similar pieces of text to be found.
